AI and machine learning improve cybersecurity by shifting detection from static, signature-based rules to adaptive, data-driven systems that identify threats at machine speed, reduce analyst workload, and automate the response steps that once took hours of manual work. Studies report a 98% threat detection rate and a 70% reduction in incident response time in AI-driven environments. That is not a marginal gain.
